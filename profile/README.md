## Good ideas deserve the chance to grow.

We're **Glean Impact** — a community of engineers, entrepreneurs, business leaders, and social impact investors putting our skills and resources to work for the common good.

We partner with local builders, incubators, and impact funds to help early-stage businesses turn meaningful ideas into lasting social impact. Our work brings together **technology, hands-on support, and purposeful capital**, with a focus on communities across Vietnam.

## Technology with a purpose

This is the home of Glean's engineering work. We build digital products that support our mission — from the **Glean marketplace** to the tools that help our team and partners serve their communities.

Our approach is practical: understand the people, build with care, and keep improving what makes a difference.
